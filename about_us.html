About Pinky

Pinky Promise is a small bussiness dedicated to making custom nail designs making each customer feel special while allowing them to express themselves in any way they choose. At Pinky Promise we have five unique artists you can choose the artist that matches your personalised style allowing you to design your own custom nail desgin.

Our Story

Pinky Promise was created from the bond a mother has with her daughters. It was created with the love for all this art and expressing that love in diffrent ways.

Our goal is to build a brand that celebrates individuality,creativity and empower women to express themselves through art and beauty.

Our Mission

To create an environment that allows women to feel creative and have fun creating their very own custom set of nails. Pinky Promise wants to bring back the creative aspect of beauty by allowing women to play around with designs of their choosing.

Our Vision

To become a trusted creative bussiness known for personalised products, execellent custom service and an overall memorable experience
